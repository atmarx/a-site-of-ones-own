# Themes

A theme controls how your site looks: the colors, the fonts, the layout of the header and footer.  Think of it as the outfit your content wears.

## Your content is separate from your theme

This is the most reassuring thing about WordPress: your pages, posts, and images are stored separately from the theme that displays them.  Switching themes changes how everything looks, but it never deletes or damages what you have written.  You can try a different theme, decide you hate it, and switch back — your content just changes clothes.

## The themes we offer

Your site comes with four themes installed, and you can switch among them at any time under **Appearance**:

- **Twenty Twenty-Five** — the theme built and maintained by WordPress itself.  Clean, simple, and hard to get wrong.  If you never think about themes at all, this is what you have, and it is a perfectly good choice.
- **Astra** — the most widely used theme in the world, with a large library of ready-made starting layouts.
- **Kadence** — similar in spirit to Astra, with especially flexible control over headers, fonts, and colors.
- **GeneratePress** — minimal and very fast.  If you want the site to get out of the way of your content, choose this one.

Any of the four will make a handsome research group site.  If you cannot decide, keep the default and spend the time on your photographs instead — it will do more good.

## Why only four?

Every theme we offer is one we have committed to keep working for years.  Themes are software: they receive updates, and when a theme's developer loses interest or goes out of business, sites built on it eventually break.  The four themes above are maintained by large, established teams with millions of users, and we keep all of them updated centrally on every site we host.

You may run across websites selling "science lab" or "laboratory" themes with impressive demonstration sites.  We looked at them closely and chose not to offer them.  Most are built on page-builder tools that trap your content in a format only that theme understands, many come from one-person shops that stop updating them, and the licenses expire annually per site.  Here is the secret about those impressive demos: what makes them look like lab sites is a nice people grid, a publications list, and good photographs — all things your site already does.  A great lab site comes from your photos and your words, not from an exotic theme.

## Making a theme yours

Within any theme you can adjust colors, fonts, and the header image from the theme's settings under **Appearance**.  If you want your site to match your department's colors, or you have something specific in mind and cannot find the knob for it, write to us — small adjustments are usually quick, and we would rather help than have you fight the settings screen for an afternoon.
